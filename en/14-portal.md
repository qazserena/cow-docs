## Card Package & NFT detail (`/profile`)

- All your NFTs by category: cattle, boxes, skins, badges, planets, vouchers, avatars  
- Detail page: stats (base / combat / milk), parents, stars, lifespan; **Sell / Transfer / Open / Rent**  
- Normal cattle without Sell / Transfer buttons, or failing transfers, are on-chain limits — see [13](./13-market.md)  
- **Planet leasing** (`/cardPackage/PanletLease`): Home leaders lease leadership monthly, tenants pay and renew — see [08](./08-guild.md)

## Voucher Center & Game Store

- **Game Store**: buy vouchers with IRT / IRG (calf, HP potion, battery, EXP card, skin box…)  
- **Voucher Center**: redeem held vouchers (including Halo / Genesis / calf vouchers from CattleMart) for the asset in one click; a small IRT fee per voucher may apply; adult-cattle and calf vouchers cannot be redeemed in the same batch  
- Approve the voucher contract first; redeemed items go to the game backpack, redeemed cattle must be put in the shed

## Guild Advancement Program (`/guild`)

For community builders: create a community of ≥ 30 (WeChat / TG…), have members claim beta assets, raise a calf to adult, and post combat / milk screenshots with #CowGalaxy; the leader submits the valid addresses. Rewards in four phases by new members and valid addresses: **100 / 500 / 1000 / 2000 USDT** plus Planet Merit / President badge NFTs; qualified leaders can claim a beta planet (named after the community). Details and quotas follow the page.

---

## Airdrop (`/airdrop`)

Claim tokens / NFTs by list: opens the first working day of the month after the announcement, closes a week later; unclaimed = forfeited. The menu entry is hidden until a formal airdrop opens; the path is reachable directly. The beta-contribution mainnet airdrop is described in [17](./17-beta.md).

---

## General portal steps

1. Connect the same wallet address as the game  
2. Switch to the right chain (the page prompts)  
3. Approve tokens / NFTs as prompted  
4. Confirm the transaction; arrival may lag  
5. Refresh or re-login in the game client to see assets
