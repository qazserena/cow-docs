# 01 Getting Started

## What you need before you start

1. A **crypto wallet** (MetaMask recommended — desktop browser extension or mobile app)  
2. A little **native gas token** in the wallet (for on-chain actions; on testnets, claim test tokens)  
3. Network access to the game client and the portal  

No wallet yet? Install MetaMask → create a wallet → **back up your seed phrase** (store it only where you control it).

---

## First time in the game

### 1. Open the game and connect your wallet

1. Open the Legend Ranch game page  
2. Click connect wallet (MetaMask)  
3. Confirm the connection and sign-in in your wallet  

Sign-in does not transfer funds — it only proves “this address is yours.” If the same account logs in elsewhere, the old session is kicked offline.

### 2. Choose your language

The game supports Simplified Chinese, English, Traditional Chinese, Korean, and more. Switch on the login page or in settings.

### 3. Bind a planet guild (very important)

After entering, you must **choose and bind a planet guild**:

- Binding is **permanent** — you cannot switch later  
- When you claim milk or combat IRT rewards, tax is auto-deducted at that planet’s rate  
- Tax counts toward your guild contribution  

When picking a planet, check: tax rate (often about 10%–20%), guild activity, and whether you can join guild battles easily.

### 4. Get your first cattle

Without cattle you can barely play the core loop. Ways to get one:

| Method | Notes |
|---|---|
| Open a blind box | Open cattle boxes at the Breeding Institute; or portal IGO / mart events (odds: [16](./16-blind-box.md)) |
| Buy on the NFT market | Buy **calves / Genesis cattle** on the portal marketplace (Normal adults cannot be listed; see [13](./13-market.md)) |
| Beta / event claims | Whitelisted users can claim on portal pages such as “Beta Assets” |
| Referral rewards | Referrers may get boxes when conditions are met |

### 5. Put cattle in the CowShed

Cattle must enter the **CowShed** for normal growth, breeding, milk, and combat.  
You start with a few stalls; expand when you need more (see [03 CowShed & Growth](./03-cowshed-and-growth.md)).

### 6. Pick your main path

| You prefer… | Suggested route |
|---|---|
| Steady token income | Raise **cows** → stake at the Milk Factory |
| Competitive combat | Raise **bulls** → Arena PVE / PVP |
| Breeding for value | Pair bulls and cows → breed and open boxes at the Breeding Institute |
| Social / guild play | Rank up, pay tax, join guild battles, claim benefits |

Most players run all three in parallel: one or two cows for milk, one or two bulls for fights, and breeding when they have spare capacity.

## What the main UI looks like

(Names as shown in-game)

- **Top / corners**: token balances (IRT, IRG); avatar opens profile  
- **Scene buildings**: CowShed, Milk Factory, Breeding Institute, Academy, Arena, Guild, Marketplace, etc.  
- **Side bar**: chat, friends, quests, check-in, backpack, mail, Reward Center  

Click buildings for each mode; use the side bar for daily claims and social.

---

## Recommended first-week order

1. Log in and bind a planet  
2. Get at least 1–2 cattle and put them in the CowShed  
3. Feed forage until calves become adults (or buy adults)  
4. Send cows to the Milk Factory; take bulls through a few Arena PVE fights  
5. Check in daily, do quests, claim from the Reward Center  
6. Learn guild benefits and tax rates; then breeding, star-up, and the tech tree when ready  

---

## Testnet vs production

- **Testnet**: assets have no real value — for practice  
- **Production**: assets have real value — confirm network, contracts, and amounts before acting  

Switching networks, claiming test tokens, whitelist claims, etc. are covered in portal docs or ops announcements.

---

## Safety reminders

- Officials will never ask for your seed phrase / private key  
- When claiming, listing, or opening boxes needs a second wallet confirmation, read the contract and amount before approving  
- Phishing sites are common: stick to official domains and announcement channels
