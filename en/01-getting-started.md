# 01 Getting Started

## What you need

1. **A crypto wallet**: the game client supports injected wallets such as MetaMask and TokenPocket; the portal also supports WalletConnect and BitKeep  
2. A little **native token for gas** (beta runs on Sepolia testnet — test ETH is free from faucets)  
3. Network access to the game client (ranch.cowgalaxy.com) and the portal (cowgalaxy.com)  

No wallet yet? Install MetaMask → create a wallet → **back up the seed phrase** (store it only where you control).

---

## Entering the game for the first time

### 1. Open the game and connect a wallet

1. Open the Legend Ranch game page  
2. Click Connect Wallet  
3. Confirm the connection and the sign-in signature in your wallet  

Signing in does not transfer anything; it only proves "this address is yours". One account may be online in one place only — logging in elsewhere kicks the old session.  
The game, the portal, and the forum share the same wallet address and data.

### 2. Choose a language

Simplified Chinese, English, Traditional Chinese and Korean are supported; switch on the login page or in Settings (restart required).

### 3. Bind a planet guild (very important)

After entering you must **choose and bind a planet guild**. Putting cattle into the CowShed requires a bound planet.

- Binding **cannot be changed by yourself** (being kicked by the leader, or founding your own guild with a planet card, are the only other paths)  
- Taxable income (milk, combat) is taxed at the planet **rate** when claimed; the tax counts toward your **guild contribution**  
- The rate is fixed by planet type and the **leader cannot change it**: Home 20%, Frontier 10%, Federation members pay the mother Home's 20%  

When choosing: rate (Frontier is lighter), guild activity, and whether it can fight guild battles (only Home planets can sign up). Some guilds require **join approval** — apply first, then confirm after the leader approves (see [08](./08-guild.md)).  
You can also found your own: an address holding a Home / Frontier planet card can create a guild and become leader directly — the beta starter pack includes a Home card.

### 4. Get your first cattle

| Method | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Beta starter pack** (recommended now) | Portal "Beta → Claim Beta Assets", one click (delivered in three transactions): Home planet, Genesis cattle, adult bull, adult cow, calf, box, a full item set, 2000 TIRT, 20000 TIRG — see [17](./17-beta.md) |
| Open a box | Open cattle boxes at the Breeding Institute, or portal IGO / CattleMart boxes (odds in [16](./16-blind-box.md)) |
| NFT marketplace | Buy **calves / Genesis cattle** (normal adults cannot be listed, see [13](./13-market.md)) |
| Referral reward | Every 5 breedings by your referee earns the referrer 1 embryo box + 1 breed card |

### 5. Put cattle into the CowShed

Cattle must be in the **CowShed** to be fed, bred, staked, or fight.  
You start with 2 slots; expand with IRT at the Academy, up to 16 (see [03](./03-cowshed-and-growth.md)).

### 6. Pick your main line

| You prefer… | Suggested route |
|---|---|
| Steady token income | Raise **cows** → stake at the Milk Factory (200,000 IRT daily pool server-wide) |
| Competitive combat | Raise **bulls** → Arena PVE / Match / Ladders |
| Breeding for value | Get a bull + a cow → breed and open boxes |
| Social / guild | Rank up, pay tax, join guild battles, claim benefits |

Most players run all three: a cow or two for milk, a bull or two for fighting, breeding when spare.

## Main screen at a glance

- **Top / corners**: IRT and IRG balances (click to jump to a DEX), avatar → profile  
- **Scene buildings**: CowShed, Milk Factory, Academy, Breeding Institute, Guild, Arena, Marketplace  
- **Sidebar**: Check-in, Rewards Center, Quests, Backpack, Mail, Friends / Chat  

Tap a building for that gameplay; use the sidebar for daily claims and social. Red dots mark things to claim or do.

## Suggested first week

1. Log in and bind a planet (or found a guild with the starter-pack Home planet)  
2. Claim the starter pack on the portal, shed all cattle; claim the daily supply every 24 h (hay + batteries + 2000 TIRG)  
3. Feed calves hay — up to 15,000 growth per day, adult at 30,000; 2 days at the fastest  
4. Stake cows at the Milk Factory (equip a badge), charge bulls at the energy station and play PVE to learn the cards  
5. Check in, clear quests, claim the Rewards Center daily; claim Arena win chests the next day  
6. Build **farm EXP** along the way (expansion / breeding / raising / consumables) and reach **level 2** to unlock the first tech nodes (see [03](./03-cowshed-and-growth.md))  
7. Learn guild benefits and guild battles; then breeding, star-up, tech tree

## Testnet vs production

- **Testnet (beta)**: assets have no real value; your activity is recorded as an airdrop basis  
- **Production**: assets have real value; verify network, contract, and amount before acting  

Beta rules, asset claims, invites, and feedback: [17 Open Beta Guide](./17-beta.md).

## Safety

- The team will never ask for your seed phrase / private key  
- When a wallet confirmation pops up (claims, listing, opening boxes), read the contract and amount first  
- Phishing sites are common: stick to official domains (cowgalaxy.com, ranch.cowgalaxy.com, bbs.cowgalaxy.com) and announcement channels
