# 15 FAQ

Look up by “what I’m seeing.” If you’re still stuck, keep screenshots, time, and wallet address (never share private keys) and contact official support / community admins.

***

## Login & account

**Can’t connect wallet / sign fails?**\
Check that MetaMask is unlocked, the network is correct, and the browser isn’t blocking pop-ups.

**Remote login warning / sudden disconnect?**\
One account can only be online in one place; logging in elsewhere kicks the old session.

***

## Cattle & CowShed

**Why can’t this cattle milk / fight?**\
Check: adult?, correct gender?, in the shed?, enough vitality?, conflicting state (staked / combat / listed / feeding / CD / dead)?

**Normal cattle died?**\
Lifespan end or long neglect causes death and burn. Use Life Potion early and keep them fed in the shed.

**Not enough stalls?**\
Expand at the Academy; clear stalls occupied by dead cattle.

***

## Milk

**Rewards too low / zero?**\
Confirm stake succeeded, vitality isn’t empty, and you tapped claim; rewards also depend on server hashrate, your stats, ranch level, badges, and tax rate.

**Balance short after claim?**\
Planet tax was auto-deducted — see [08](../combat-and-guilds/08-guild.md).

***

## Combat

**Can’t find a match?**\
Timeout may match bots; or you’ve used up today’s entries / lack energy.

**Why can’t I join guild battle?**\
You must be on the leader’s roster and in the battle stage; check whether the guild signed up.

***

## Guild benefits

**Leader sent daily benefits but can’t see them?**\
Leaders don’t share in daily benefits — expected.

**Members can’t see them either?**\
Confirm distribute succeeded, not expired, member didn’t join today, and their rank has a share.

**Can’t claim plunder benefits?**\
Need a guild battle win this week and leader distribution; no win → no reward prompt.

***

## Breeding

**Can’t breed?**\
Check counts, CD, vitality, fee balance, state; rentals also need the listed cattle still available.

**Opened a box with no Genesis?**\
Genesis is extremely rare — normal RNG.

***

## Claims & on-chain

**Tapped Reward Center but wallet didn’t receive?**\
Many rewards need a second on-chain confirm; check whether the tx succeeded, network is correct, and it’s still pending.

**Gas failed / stuck?**\
Raise Gas or wait out congestion; don’t resubmit many txs with different params.

**Is approving dangerous?**\
Only approve official contracts; periodically review and revoke unknown approvals in a block explorer.

***

## Market

**Can’t find a certain cattle?**\
May be delisted, death-filtered, filters too strict, or a network error.

**Listing failed?**\
No approval, unsellable state, not enough Gas balance, or network error.
