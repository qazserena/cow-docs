## The Game in One Sentence

Legend Ranch is a blockchain game built around **cattle NFTs**:

1. Log in with your wallet and join a planet guild
2. Open blind boxes or buy on the market to get cattle
3. Put cattle in the CowShed and raise them
4. **Cows** go to the Milk Factory to mine **IRT**; **bulls** fight in the Arena for **IRG / IRT**
5. Breed, star-up, and invest in the tech tree to grow stronger
6. Join guilds and guild battles, and claim tax benefits

Assets (cattle, blind boxes, skins, badges, planets, items) are mostly on-chain NFTs / tokens and can circulate on the market.

## Background Story (optional)

In the CowGalaxy universe, cattle form two great interstellar federations by belief — the **Galactic Empire** and the **Explorer Alliance**.\
Planets fall into three types: Home, Federation, and Frontier. Players build ranches on planets, raise cattle, wage war, and compete for resources and honor.
