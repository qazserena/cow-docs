## The game in one breath

Interstellar Rangeland is a blockchain game built around **cattle NFTs**:

1. Log in with a wallet and join a planet guild  
2. Get cattle by opening boxes or buying on the marketplace (during beta: claim the starter pack)  
3. Put them in the CowShed and raise them  
4. **Cows** go to the Milk Factory to mine **IRT**; **bulls** go to the Arena to earn **IRG / IRT**  
5. Breed, star-up, and research the tech tree to get stronger  
6. Join guilds and guild battles, claim tax benefits  

Assets (cattle, boxes, skins, badges, planets, items, avatars) are on-chain NFTs / tokens, most of them tradable.

## Background (optional)

In the CowGalaxy, cattle split by belief into two interstellar federations — the **Galactic Empire** and the **Explorer Alliance**.  
Planets come in three kinds: Home, Federation, and Frontier. Players build ranches, raise cattle, and fight for resources and glory.

## Important notes

- Open Beta runs on testnet; beta assets will not migrate to mainnet. Beta contributions are converted and distributed at mainnet launch — see [17](./17-beta.md).  
- Keep your private key safe; the team will never ask for your seed phrase or private key.  
- Most on-chain actions require an **approve** first, then a transaction; read the contract and amount before confirming.
