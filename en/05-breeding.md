# 05 Breeding Institute

The Breeding Institute handles **breeding**: pair a bull and a cow to get an **embryo blind box (cattle blind box)**, then open it for new cattle or shards.

## Breed with your own cattle

### Requirements

- One available **bull** + one available **cow**  
- Both still have remaining **breed counts** (common cap: 5 each)  
- Not on CD, enough vitality, not dead / not in a conflicting state  
- Pay the breeding fee (**IRT + IRG**; fee tiers by breed count, adjustable; spend is usually burned)  

### Flow

1. Open the **Breeding Institute**  
2. Choose “Breed / Select for breeding”  
3. Pick bull and cow, confirm the fee  
4. On success you get a blind box; both cattle enter breeding CD  
5. Open the box in the open-box UI  

### How offspring tend to work

- Some shared stats relate to the parents’ average, then a random factor  
- Bull combat stats lean toward the father; cow milk stats lean toward the mother  
- Caps and floors prevent fully absurd results  

Genesis cattle can use **Breed Cards** for extra breed counts; Normal cattle usually cannot break the same limits with Breed Cards (see item text).

## Breeding rental market (rent cattle to breed)

When you don’t want to own both genders, or want to rent out idle breeders:

### List for rent

1. Choose the cattle to rent out  
2. Set the breeding fee  
3. List on the rental market  

Common reasons you can’t list: low vitality, 0 breed counts, dead, on CD, conflicting state.

### Rent someone else’s cattle

1. Filter the rental market by bloodline / gender / counts / price, etc.  
2. Pay the breeding fee to complete the rental breed  
3. The platform usually takes a fee (e.g. about 2%)  

After a deal, the listing is taken down.

## Opening blind boxes

Open breeding cattle boxes at the Breeding Institute. Initial odds:

- **Normal calf 90%**
- **Cattle shards 9.99%** (4 or 5 per open)
- **Genesis cattle 0.01%** (1 in 10,000)

**10** shards craft a Normal cattle at the Academy.

Full tables for IGO, CattleMart, and skin boxes: **[16 Blind box odds](./16-blind-box.md)**.

## Referrals / invites and breeding perks

If you bound a referrer (who must hold at least one cattle):

- Each time a friend successfully breeds a set number of boxes (commonly every 5), the referrer may get an **embryo box + Breed Card**, etc. (auto on-chain)  

The portal also has beta invite events (complete tasks for USDT) — see [14](./14-portal.md).

---

## Tips

- Prefer high-stat breeders for better offspring expectations  
- Watch CD and breed counts — don’t lock key combat / milk cattle in cooldown  
- Before listing for rent, check fees and market prices
