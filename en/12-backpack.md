## Skins

Skins are ERC721 NFTs worn on cattle, changing the combat look and adding stats (attack / defense / stamina). Bulls and cows wear separately; if a worn skin is transferred away, it is unequipped on the next entry.

### Five regular skins

| Skin | Level | Shop price (USD-equivalent, IRT or USDT) |
|---|---|---|
| Baby Bomber (战争量产机) | 1 | 625 |
| Evil Blaze Dryad (恶焰德鲁伊) | 1 | 625 |
| Space Explorer (星际探索者) | 2 | 935 |
| Electric-Arc Spirit (电弧灵体) | 2 | 935 |
| Holo Electro-Magnetizer (全息电磁帮) | 3 | 1250 |

- The skin shop sells 1000 of each, **one per skin per address**; skin boxes, the guild shop, vouchers, and IGO skin packs are not limited  
- Skin bonuses apply in the Arena and guild battle  

### Crafting the ultimate skin "Mecha Demon"

At the skin shop, combine **3 regular skins** (any mix):

- Success chance = sum of skin levels: level 1 6%, level 2 8%, level 3 10% → 18 – 30%  
- Success gives **Mecha Demon (0★)**; on failure all three burn and 1 random regular skin is returned (10% Holo, 30% level 2, 60% level 1)  
- Batch crafting by level is supported (up to 50 sets)  

### Ultimate skin star-up

3 Mecha Demons of the **same star** → next star:

| Star-up | Success | On failure |
|---|---|---|
| 0 → 1 | 70% | 2 burned, 1 kept |
| 1 → 2 | 50% | 2 burned, 1 kept |
| 2 → 3 | 30% | 2 burned, 1 kept |

No IRT compensation. Entry: Arena skin select, profile, shop.

|---|---|---|---|

## Tips

1. Hay first for milking and fighting cattle; a calf's hay all becomes growth  
2. Save EXP cards for calves just short of adulthood (and bank the farm EXP)  
3. HP potions are expensive (4000 IRT+); extend Normal cattle via star-up instead  
4. Use pass / protection cards within the daily limit  
5. Equip badges early — idle badges are lost hashpower  

Shops: Academy, guild shop, portal Voucher Center / Game Store.
