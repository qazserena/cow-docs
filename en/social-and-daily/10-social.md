# 10 Friends & Social

## Friends

1. Search for a player and send a request
2. After they accept, you’re friends
3. You can private chat and invite to Match parties, etc.

Entry is usually the main side bar “Friends.”

***

## Chat channels

Common channels:

| Channel           | Use                                                 |
| ----------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| Guild             | Planet members chat, party up, guild battle command |
| Friend DM         | One-to-one                                          |
| Federation planet | Federation-related members                          |

Chat has sensitive-word filters; blocked content may not send.

***

## Intimacy bonds (on-chain)

Four relationship types (not strictly tied to gender):

* Close friends
* Couples
* Besties
* Bros

### How to reach bindable intimacy

1. Party with a friend in combat to build intimacy (e.g. +2 per match)
2. At the threshold (e.g. 200), send a bind request
3. After both agree, **bind on-chain** (wallet action required)

### Benefits of binding

* Party combat may get an extra reward multiplier (e.g. about ×1.2 — trust in-game)
* Social display and achievement feel

Unbind / change rules follow the game and contracts — read prompts before acting.

***

## Party tips

* Match 3v3 / 5v5 can invite from friends or guild chat
* Guild quests often require “complete in a party”
* When intimate friends get better party rewards, queue together
