# 12 Backpack · Items · Skins · Badges

## Backpack

Entry: main side bar “Backpack.”\
Common tabs: **Forage / Items / Other / Skins / Badges**.

In the backpack you can view, use, and sometimes craft. Using some items also adds ranch XP.

***

## Skins

* **Get**: skin shop (IRT / USDT), skin boxes, events, craft
* **Wear**: bulls / cows separately; affect combat look and stats (ATK / DEF / stamina / HP, etc.)
* **Craft**: multiple common skins can craft an ultimate skin; ultimates can star-up (failure may refund some IRT)

Entry: Arena skin select, profile, shops.

***

## Badges

Badges are ERC721, mainly equipped at the **Milk Factory** to boost milk hashrate (usually only **one** at a time).

### Craft chain (concept)

* Badge shards → Bronze (chance-based)
* Bronze → Silver → Gold → Platinum (success rate falls; failure may partially refund)
* Diamond / Glory are mostly high-tier events or draws

Hashrate numbers: [04 Milk Factory](../growth-and-earnings/04-milk-factory.md).

***

## Usage tips

1. Prioritize forage for milk and combat cattle
2. Save growth packs for key calves near adulthood
3. Use Clear / Protection cards when pushing daily tiers or boards
4. Equip badges early — idle badges waste hashrate

Related shops: Academy, guild shop, portal store.
