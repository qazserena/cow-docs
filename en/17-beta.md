# 17 Open Beta Guide · Packs · Invite Points · Airdrop

**Beta window: October 1 – December 31, 2026**, on Sepolia testnet, all gameplay free.

The point of beta is finding problems: every session and every report is recorded as a basis for the mainnet airdrop. The portal "Beta" menu is laid out in play order: **read the rules → claim assets → invite → report**.

---

## 1. Claim beta assets (portal `/betaAssets`)

Connect and sign in, then two tiers are available. Claims are authorised by an official voucher (valid 10 minutes) and land on-chain in a single transaction.

### Starter pack (once per wallet)

Opens every door of the game:

| Asset | Amount | For |
|---|---|---|
| Home planet card | 1 | Found a guild and lead it, eligible for guild battle right away (population 10,000, tax 20%, see [08](./08-guild.md)) |
| Genesis cattle | 1 | High stats, eternal, tradable |
| Adult bull / adult cow | 1 each | Bull for the Arena, cow for milk; breed them together |
| Normal calf | 1 | Experience the feed → adult path |
| Cattle box | 1 | Open at the Breeding Institute |
| Hay / Prime / Supreme | 10 each | Feeding |
| EXP Card normal / prime / supreme | 3 each | Speed up the calf |
| STR Battery normal / prime / supreme | 3 each | Energy / combat energy |
| HP Potion normal / prime / supreme | 1 each | Extend Normal cattle |
| TIRT | 2000 | Expansion, tech, breeding seed money |
| TIRG | 20000 | Hay |

The pack doesn't promise "enough for the whole beta"; the in-game loop (milk, win chests, quests) takes over.

### Daily supply (every 24 hours)

| Asset | Amount |
|---|---|
| Hay / Prime / Supreme | 10 each |
| STR Battery normal / prime / supreme | 2 each |
| TIRG | 2000 |

Consumables only — no NFTs, no TIRT — so bot farms can't skew the airdrop. Conditions:

- You have **entered the game at least once** (wallet logged into the game)  
- You have **completed at least one in-game quest** (real play like staking a cow or a few PVE matches)  
- A cap on wallets claiming from one network in a short window blocks bulk alts  

A greyed button explains why: enter the game first / finish a quest / cooling down / out of stock (wait for restock).

## 2. Invite for points (portal `/betaInvite`)

Each address has a **12-character invite code** and link (generated on first visit, permanent). When friends arrive via your link or enter your code, points accrue as below. **Points are a core weight of the mainnet airdrop.**

### Point rules

| Event | Who earns | Points |
|---|---|---|
| Code bound successfully | Invitee | +30 |
| A friend binds to you | Inviter | +20 |
| The friend enters the game | Inviter | +50 |
| The friend completes a first quest (= **valid invite**) | Inviter | +100 |
| Valid invites reach 5 / 10 / 25 / 50 / 100 | Inviter | +200 / +500 / +1500 / +4000 / +10000 |

A fully converted friend is worth 170 points, milestones extra.

### Levels (display only, not part of airdrop settlement)

| Level | Title | Points |
|---|---|---|
| Lv1 | Calf | 0 |
| Lv2 | Rancher | 300 |
| Lv3 | Herder | 1,200 |
| Lv4 | Ranch Boss | 3,000 |
| Lv5 | Star Pioneer | 8,000 |
| Lv6 | Galaxy Legend | 20,000 |
| Lv7 | Cosmic Overlord | 50,000 |

### Rules and anti-abuse

- Each address binds an inviter **once**, permanently; you can't use your own code or bind back to someone you invited  
- If the invitee's network matches one the inviter used in the last 30 days, or that network already has 3 scoring binds in 30 days → the relation is recorded but **neither side scores** (marked "not scored")  
- Points come only from real testnet play; bots, scripts, and alt farms are removed and may lose points  
- Leaderboard shows the Top 50 (ties by time), refreshed about every minute; the page offers an invite poster and one-click share to X

## 3. Airdrop rules (portal `/beta`)

No tokens are issued during beta. Three months of contribution are converted and distributed **at mainnet launch** (tokens + items) across three dimensions:

| Dimension | What counts |
|---|---|
| **Bug reports** | Number and severity of accepted issues, first valid report wins; valuable gameplay / balance suggestions count too |
| **Interaction data** | Breadth and persistence across raising, milk, combat, guild battle, trading — not grinding one thing |
| **Community** | Forum posts and replies, helping players, guides and content |

Invite points sit alongside these three. Final weights, ratios, and the list are announced before distribution.

## 4. Feedback & community (forum bbs.cowgalaxy.com)

Wallet-signature login, same address as the game. Categories:

| Category | Notes |
|---|---|
| Game Discussion | Gameplay, guides |
| Game Planning | Suggestions for future versions |
| **Bugs & Feedback** | **Visible only to you and admins**, so nobody exploits posted bugs; the portal "Feedback & Community" link opens this category |
| Other | — |

For bug posts include: which screen, how to reproduce, your wallet address (never the private key). Anti-spam: one post per 30 s and one reply per 10 s per address.

## 5. Things to know first

- Beta runs on testnet. Tokens, cattle, planets, and items earned in beta **do not migrate to mainnet**; they are for trying the gameplay  
- To tune the economy, numbers may change and some data may be reset at any time during beta; follow current announcements  
- Script farming and alt armies are excluded from the airdrop list  
- This chapter is interim; final airdrop rules follow the official announcement before mainnet launch
